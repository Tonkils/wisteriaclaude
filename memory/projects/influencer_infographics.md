Creator performance infographic (861px wide, vertical): header handle, subtitle, 3 KPI boxes (Total Views, New Followers, Engagement Rate), Engagement vs Benchmarks bars with "Nx platform average" badge, Viral Engine panel (top post + interactions/shares/saves), 3-column performance table, content pillars, footer tagline + DM CTA + sources. Reference: @glitzndabz June 2026 (207.8K views, 198 followers, 6.79% ER).

**Formulas (verified against the glitz image where possible):**
- Total views = sum of the Views column of the Meta Business Suite posts export (glitz columns 95.7K+93.2K+18.9K = 207.8K).
- New followers = sum of the Follows column (post-attributed, not net growth; glitz 66+129+3 = 198).
- "Nx" = ER / 0.48% (IG platform avg). Benchmarks: IG avg 0.48%, cannabis macro 1.5%, cannabis micro 3.0% (Socialinsider 2026 / MediaJel, taken from the glitz graphic, not independently verified).
- Glitz's 6.79% ER could not be reproduced from its own displayed numbers, so its method is unknown.
- Table columns = three equal 30-day periods; reach is summed per post (not unique reach).

**@thematchadragon (Jul 24–Oct 21, 2026, 25.5K followers as of Oct 23):** 985.8K views, 2,007 post-attributed follows, per-follower ER 6.32% (avg interactions per post / followers; 13.2x IG avg). Median post ER 0.92%; 4.85% without the top post (Super GALS, 169.4K views). Last 30 days dropped to 101.8K views / 105 follows.

**Why:** Per-follower ER chosen by Jaret because the benchmarks are follower-based; a view-based rate (14.07%) would overstate the comparison. Audience framing is cannabis brands, like glitz.

**How to apply:** For future creator infographics, ask for current follower count, use the same formulas, state the ER method in the footer, and flag that tagline/pillars are placeholders until the creator approves. Files in `infographics/`.
